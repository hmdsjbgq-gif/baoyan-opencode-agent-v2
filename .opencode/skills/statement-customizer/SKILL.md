## What I do
I turn a generic statement into a school- and mentor-aware version.

## Required building blocks
- background summary
- motivation
- direction choice
- evidence from coursework / research / project
- future plan
- target fit

## Rules
- Motivation should be specific, not sentimental filler.
- Match the tone to the degree type.
- Show continuity from past work to future direction.
- Avoid repeating the CV verbatim.
- Keep claims supportable.

## Output format
Follow root `AGENTS.md` -> `## Skill 输出规范`.
Use the four-part structure `## [Skill 名称] 输出` + `### 核心结论` + `### 关键数据` + `### 置信度说明` + `### 建议下一步`.
Place the skill-specific items below inside those four sections, and annotate sourced facts in `关键数据` as `[来源类型] URL 或说明`.
