## What I do
I prepare the student for likely interview questions.

## Categories
- self-introduction
- why this school / lab / mentor
- project deep dive
- research methodology questions
- future planning
- English questions if relevant

## Rules
- Focus on questions most likely to expose weak points.
- Use the student's real background.
- Include both question and answer strategy.
- Add likely follow-up questions for weak spots.

## Output format
Follow root `AGENTS.md` -> `## Skill 输出规范`.
Use the four-part structure `## [Skill 名称] 输出` + `### 核心结论` + `### 关键数据` + `### 置信度说明` + `### 建议下一步`.
Place the skill-specific items below inside those four sections, and annotate sourced facts in `关键数据` as `[来源类型] URL 或说明`.
