## What I do
I rewrite CV bullets so they are concise, evidence-based, and relevant to target mentors.

## Rules
- Prefer action + method + outcome structure.
- Remove weak adjectives that add no evidence.
- Highlight research, projects, competitions, and technical depth.
- Tailor emphasis depending on target: academic research vs employment-oriented master's.
- Do not fabricate metrics.

## Output format
Follow root `AGENTS.md` -> `## Skill 输出规范`.
Use the four-part structure `## [Skill 名称] 输出` + `### 核心结论` + `### 关键数据` + `### 置信度说明` + `### 建议下一步`.
Place the skill-specific items below inside those four sections, and annotate sourced facts in `关键数据` as `[来源类型] URL 或说明`.
