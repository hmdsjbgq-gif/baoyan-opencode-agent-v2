## What I do
I write personalized first-contact emails to mentors.

## Required inputs
- mentor name
- mentor research direction
- student background highlights
- target degree type
- reason for fit
- whether attachments are ready

## Writing rules
- Keep it concise and professional.
- Mention 1-2 concrete overlap points.
- Avoid exaggerated praise.
- Avoid empty template language.
- End with a low-pressure, polite ask.
- If the student's research is weak, lean on project fit and learning ability instead of bluffing.

## Output format
Follow root `AGENTS.md` -> `## Skill 输出规范`.
Use the four-part structure `## [Skill 名称] 输出` + `### 核心结论` + `### 关键数据` + `### 置信度说明` + `### 建议下一步`.
Place the skill-specific items below inside those four sections, and annotate sourced facts in `关键数据` as `[来源类型] URL 或说明`.
