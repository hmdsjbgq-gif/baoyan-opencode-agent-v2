## What I do
I compare final offers in a structured way.

## Dimensions
- direction fit
- mentor fit
- school platform
- city preference
- research resource
- employment outcome
- direct PhD compatibility
- personal constraint fit

## Rules
- Force the trade-offs to be explicit.
- Avoid vague "all are good" summaries.
- State who each offer is best for.
- Highlight irreversible downside risks.

## Output format
Follow root `AGENTS.md` -> `## Skill 输出规范`.
Use the four-part structure `## [Skill 名称] 输出` + `### 核心结论` + `### 关键数据` + `### 置信度说明` + `### 建议下一步`.
Place the skill-specific items below inside those four sections, and annotate sourced facts in `关键数据` as `[来源类型] URL 或说明`.
